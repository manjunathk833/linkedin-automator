# GST Letter of Undertaking (LUT) Form RFD-11 & Export of Services Guide

**Target Audience:** Senior SDET / Remote Software Contractor (~6.8 YOE)  
**Corridor:** International Remote Clients (US / EU / Global)  
**Governing Statute:** Section 16 & Section 2(6) of IGST Act, 2017 read with Rule 96A of CGST Rules, 2017  
**Filing Portal:** GST Portal (`gst.gov.in`) $\rightarrow$ Services $\rightarrow$ User Services $\rightarrow$ Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT)  

---

## 1. Statutory Registration Threshold

Under Section 22 of the CGST Act, 2017, any service provider whose aggregate turnover exceeds **₹20,00,000** in a financial year must register for GST.  
Even though export of software testing / development services is taxed at **0%**, obtaining a GSTIN is **mandatory** once turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs.

---

## 2. Statutory Conditions for "Export of Services"

Under **Section 2(6) of the IGST Act, 2017**, your remote engagement qualifies as an export of service if and only if all 5 conditions are fulfilled:

1. **Location of Supplier:** The supplier of service is located in India.
2. **Location of Recipient:** The recipient of service (US client / startup) is located outside India.
3. **Place of Supply:** The place of supply of service is outside India (determined by Section 13(2) of the IGST Act as the location of the recipient).
4. **Convertible Foreign Exchange:** Payment is received in convertible foreign exchange (or Indian Rupees wherever permitted by RBI, e.g. through authorized AD Code banking corridors like Wise / SWIFT).
5. **Distinct Entities:** The supplier and recipient are not merely establishments of a distinct person.

---

## 3. Letter of Undertaking (LUT) Form RFD-11

### 3.1 Purpose & Benefit
Under **Section 16(3) of the IGST Act** and **Rule 96A of the CGST Rules**, exporters have two options:
- **Option A (Without LUT):** Pay 18% Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST) upfront on every foreign invoice, and claim a refund later (locks up working capital).
- **Option B (With LUT - Recommended):** Furnish a **Letter of Undertaking (Form GST RFD-11)** online, allowing you to **export services at 0% IGST without paying any upfront tax**.

### 3.2 Step-by-Step Filing Procedure
1. Log in to the GST Portal (`www.gst.gov.in`).
2. Navigate to: **Services** $\rightarrow$ **User Services** $\rightarrow$ **Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT)**.
3. Select Financial Year (e.g., `2026-2027`).
4. Enter details of **two independent witnesses** (Name, Address, Occupation).
5. Sign and file using **Electronic Verification Code (EVC)** via Aadhaar OTP.
6. The GST portal generates an **Application Reference Number (ARN)** instantly. Download and preserve the LUT acknowledgment PDF.
7. **Validity:** An LUT remains valid for the entire financial year (April 1 to March 31). File a fresh LUT in April of each new FY.

## 4. Mandatory Foreign Invoice Format (Rule 46)

Every invoice raised to an overseas client must carry statutory export declarations.

### 4.1 Required Invoice Elements
- **Header:** `TAX INVOICE`
- **Mandatory Endorsement (Must appear at top or bottom):**
  > *"SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT OF SERVICES UNDER LETTER OF UNDERTAKING (LUT) WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"*
- **LUT ARN:** Reference Number and Date of filing.
- **Supplier Details:** Legal Name, Residential/Office Address, PAN, GSTIN.
- **Client Details:** Client Company Name, US/Overseas Headquarters Address, State/Country.
- **Services Description:** Software Engineering / Automated Test Infrastructure Consulting.
- **Service Accounting Code (SAC):** **998314** (IT design and development services) or **998313** (IT consulting services).
- **Billing Amount:** Stated in USD ($) and converted INR equivalent based on RBI reference rate of the invoice date.
- **Bank & Remittance Details:** Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC, SWIFT Code, and Authorized Dealer (AD) Code.

## 5. Routine Return Filing Compliance

| GST Return | Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **GSTR-1** | Monthly / Quarterly (QRMP) | Report export invoices in **Table 6A (Export Invoices)** under sub-category *"Export without payment of tax (WOP)"*. Cite invoice number, date, and USD/INR value. |
| **GSTR-3B** | Monthly / Quarterly (QRMP) | Report total gross receipts in **Table 3.1(b) (Zero-rated supplies)**. Tax payable: **₹0**. |
